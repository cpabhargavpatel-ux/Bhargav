Put your videos / audio files in this folder (sub-folders are fine).

MP4 (H.264 + AAC) plays everywhere; WebM, MOV, MP3 and M4A also work.

Subtitles: add .srt or .vtt files next to the video with the same name:
  lesson1.mp4
  lesson1.fr.srt   <- the language you are learning
  lesson1.en.srt   <- the translation (optional)

Then open the Shadowing tab and press the refresh button.

Shadowing

Videos from your Shadowing folder.

    Pick a video on the left.

    S start / stop the video R record my voice / stop recording P play my voice / stop playing O original video from where my recording started ← → back / forward 3 seconds 0 back to the beginning L repeat the video on / off

    Shadowing: play the video and speak along with it, a split second behind, copying the rhythm, the melody and the sounds. Use headphones so the video's sound doesn't leak into your recording. On an iPhone or iPad the microphone needs the https:// address (see SETUP.md); the keyboard shortcuts need a keyboard, the buttons work everywhere.

How it works

Practice loop: it says the sentence Repeats times (set above); after each time it records you for the Record time, then shows your score. ■ Stop ends it.

Your latest recording of each sentence is saved in this browser only (see My sentences) — it isn’t uploaded anywhere by this app. The pronunciation check uses your browser’s speech recognition — Chrome or Edge — which sends the audio to the browser maker’s service to turn it into text, so it needs internet and is only approximate. In other browsers you still get the recording to listen back to. The microphone only works on localhost (or https).
